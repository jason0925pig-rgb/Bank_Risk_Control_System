How the multi-agent pipeline works

Every incoming payment passes through four specialised agents before the bank approves or declines it. Cheap agents run first and filter; the expensive judge only sees cases worth deciding.

🔍1 · Triage Agent

Screens the transaction against 90 days of history: amount vs typical spend, merchant category risk, first-time country, time of day, velocity. Emits a 0–10 suspicion score.

📍2 · Context Agent

Cross-checks the cardholder's phone GPS trail with the transaction location. Catches "impossible travel" — the card used 5,000 km away from where the phone is.

💬3 · Communication Agent

Scans recent SMS/email for phishing, urgency pressure and typosquatted domains, and links them to the payment when they precede it by under 48 h.

⚖️4 · Judge Agent

Weighs every piece of evidence into a 0–100 risk score. < 55 approve · ≥ 55 decline · ≥ 75 decline and freeze the card instantly.
